# Publications

*Journal and conference papers written during the bachelor's degree at Lovely Professional University, India, 2021 to 2022.*

**Topics:** bio-inspired aerodynamics · slotted wing tips and winglets · bell-shaped lift distribution · induced drag · low-Reynolds-number UAV design · literature review

**Tools used in the papers:** Autodesk Fusion 360 · SimScale · XFLR5

## Overview

Three of these papers come from a research group at the university that worked on the aerodynamics of bird-inspired wings for unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs). The work started with a review of the flight of the albatross, continued with the design and analysis of a UAV wing based on it, and then added a slotted winglet to that wing. The fourth paper is a single-author review on a different subject.

## 1. Aerodynamic analysis of bionic winglet-slotted wings

P. Dutta, O. P. Nagar, S. K. Sahu, R. R. Savale and R. Gokul Raj, *Materials Today: Proceedings*, 2022. [doi.org/10.1016/j.matpr.2022.04.752](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.matpr.2022.04.752)

- **Subject:** a winglet with separate slots, modelled on the slotted wing tips of eagles, added to the albatross-inspired UAV wing of the earlier paper. The aim is to reduce the wing tip vortices and the induced drag.
- **Method:** the wing and the winglet slots were modelled in Autodesk Fusion 360 and simulated with a RANS solver in SimScale at 16 m/s and an angle of attack of 0°.
- **Outcome:** the simulation gives a lift coefficient of 0.75, and the particle traces show weaker tip vortices than for a rectangular wing. The paper is a first study at one flow condition.
- **My contribution:** first and corresponding author. Conceptualisation and writing of the original draft.

## 2. Albatross and Falcon inspired Bionic UAV: An Aerodynamic Analysis

B. C. Mathew, S. K. Sahu, P. Dutta, R. Savale and J. V. Muruga Lal Jeyan, *International Journal of Aviation, Aeronautics, and Aerospace*, Vol. 8, Issue 3, 2021. [doi.org/10.15394/ijaaa.2021.1598](https://doi.org/10.15394/ijaaa.2021.1598)

- **Subject:** a UAV with a wing of 2 m span based on the wing of the albatross. The planform and the twist were chosen to give a bell-shaped lift distribution, which unloads the wing tips.
- **Method:** the wing was analysed with the vortex lattice method in XFLR5 and the full configuration with a RANS simulation in SimScale, both at 15 m/s. The wing was compared with rectangular, trapezoidal and elliptical planforms of the same area.
- **Outcome:** the analysis gives a lift coefficient of 0.65 at 0° and a maximum lift-to-drag ratio of about 20, with a lift distribution that falls to nearly zero at the tips.
- **My contribution:** co-author, third of five authors.

## 3. Evolutionary and Hereditary Traits of an Albatross and its Aerodynamic Optimality

B. C. Mathew, J. V. Muruga Lal Jeyan, P. Dutta and R. R. Savale, *IOP Conference Series: Earth and Environmental Science*, Vol. 775, 012002, 2021. [doi.org/10.1088/1755-1315/775/1/012002](https://doi.org/10.1088/1755-1315/775/1/012002)

- **Subject:** a review of the features behind the flight efficiency of the albatross: dynamic soaring, the bell-shaped lift distribution, and the morphology of the wing.
- **Outcome:** the review identifies the traits that can be transferred to UAVs with long range and endurance. It provided the background for the two papers above.
- **My contribution:** co-author, third of four authors.

## 4. Assessment of Environmental Implications of Electric Vehicles: A Review

P. Dutta, *Journal of Research in Engineering and Applied Sciences*, Vol. 6, Issue 4, October 2021.

- **Subject:** a review of the environmental effects of hybrid electric vehicles over their life cycle, covering production, operation and end of life.
- **Outcome:** the review compares impact categories such as global warming potential, acidification and human toxicity, and identifies the production phase, including the battery, and the operation phase as the main contributors.
- **My contribution:** single author.
